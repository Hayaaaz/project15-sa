Autonomy Policy for the Classroom Attendance API Service  
(August 2026)

This policy governs autonomous actions taken on the Classroom Attendance API, a service I operate and am accountable for. The autonomy ladder for this service is: Detect (read-only metrics such as API latency, error rate, and request volume), Suggest (propose configuration changes such as increasing worker threads or rotating logs), and Approve (execute storage-modifying or cost-bearing actions such as resizing the backing PostgreSQL volume or enabling premium autoscaling). No action-tier step exists for this service.

The following actions are gated: database volume resizing, enabling paid autoscaling tiers, and modifying retention policies. These require explicit approval from the on-call administrator or the service owner. Suggestions may be generated autonomously, but no approve-tier action may execute without human confirmation.

This service follows a strict Rule-of-Two: the agent may only use two tools — metrics.get() and storage.resize() — and storage.resize() always requires human approval. The agent may never combine tools in a way that produces irreversible or cost-bearing changes without human oversight.

All autonomous activity is logged through a dedicated audit pipeline: every detect, suggest, approve, and verify step is written to /var/log/attendance-api-autonomy.log with timestamps, parameters, and outcomes. Logs are retained for 180 days.

A consumption cap is enforced: no autonomous remediation may exceed $25/month in additional cloud cost. Any action projected to exceed this cap is automatically blocked and escalated.

This policy aligns with EU AI Act Article 14 (human oversight), acknowledging that enforcement timelines remain in flux as of 2026
